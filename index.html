Process / How we work together

Visible progress. Clear decisions. No mystery layer.

You communicate directly with the person designing the architecture and writing the code. Every phase ends with something concrete you can review.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We clarify the problem, users, priorities, constraints, and success criteria. You receive a practical scope with milestones, timing, and a clear pricing approach.

  2. 02

    Shape

    Flows, wireframes, and technical decisions make the product tangible before heavy development. Important assumptions are tested while changes are still inexpensive.

  3. 03

    Build

    Development happens in short, reviewable cycles. You see installable builds, working integrations, and honest trade-offs as the product grows—not only at the end.

  4. 04

    Launch

    I handle release preparation, store submission, production checks, documentation, and clean handover. Ongoing support is available when the product needs its next iteration.

FAQ / Working together

The useful questions, answered upfront.

Still deciding whether SRK Studios fits your project? These are the things clients usually want to understand first.

Ask something else ↗
What kinds of projects do you take on?+

New Flutter products, startup MVPs, features for existing applications, full-stack mobile builds, custom plugin and native work, backend integrations, and rescue or modernization work for codebases that have become difficult to ship.

How do we start a project?+

Email kornisivaramkrishna@gmail.com with a short description of the product, problem, or existing app. I’ll reply with focused questions and arrange a discovery conversation. From there, you receive a written scope covering milestones, timing, and cost.

How do you charge?+

Well-defined work can be priced by milestone. Ongoing development, maintenance, and exploratory product work can use a weekly or hourly model. The structure and expectations are agreed in writing before development begins.

Who owns the code and accounts?+

You do. Repositories, store listings, backend services, credentials, and documentation should live under your ownership wherever possible. The goal is a clean handover with no artificial lock-in.

Do you provide support after launch?+

Yes. Support can include dependency and Flutter upgrades, OS compatibility work, production fixes, monitoring, performance improvements, and new feature iterations—either on demand or through an ongoing arrangement.

Can you join an existing team or codebase?+

Yes. I can work alongside an internal team or other specialists, follow the project’s existing conventions and delivery process, contribute through code reviews, and take ownership of a defined product or engineering area.
